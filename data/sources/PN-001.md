# Practice Note PN-001 — Inclusion-timing policy wording in Jurisdiction South

**Contributed by:** Senior verifier, Jurisdiction South (identity held by the editor)
**Submitted:** 20 May 2025. **Reviewed and published:** 27 May 2025.
**Tier:** Practitioner. **Relates to:** composite inclusion timing (3.A.4 of Edition 2021; 3.A.6 of Edition 2025). **Scope:** JS.

JS examiners expect the composite policy to state the specific inclusion month.
Policies using "as soon as practicable" drew findings in 2024 examinations. Firms
preparing for examination should state the month explicitly and show that it has been
applied consistently across the composite.

*This note records practitioner experience. It is not an interpretation of the
Standards by the Board and does not modify any provision.*
