# Jurisdiction South — Adoption Notice 2024-11

**JS Standards Sponsor**
Issued 15 November 2024.

The JS Standards Sponsor, in consultation with the JS Securities Authority, has
determined that the GSB Standards, Edition 2025 will take effect in Jurisdiction
South for periods beginning on or after **1 January 2026**, one year after the global
effective date, to allow firms to align composite policies with the Authority's
examination cycle.

Until that date, firms in Jurisdiction South claiming compliance with the GSB Standards
must continue to apply the GSB Standards, Edition 2021. Firms may adopt Edition 2025
early only with the written consent of the Sponsor.
