# Guidance Statement GS-2025-02 — Composite Minimums

**Global Standards Board (GSB)**
Adopted 15 February 2025. Effective 1 March 2025.
Interprets provision 3.A.7 of the GSB Standards, Edition 2025 (provision 3.A.5 of Edition 2021).

## Purpose

Firms have asked how a documented composite minimum is to be applied over time,
and in particular when a portfolio's assets move across the minimum between
measurement periods. This Guidance Statement sets out the Board's interpretation.
It does not create new requirements.

## Guidance

Provision 3.A.7 requires that portfolios below a documented composite minimum not be
included in the composite. Firms should assess each portfolio against the minimum at
the beginning of each measurement period, using the valuation methodology documented
in the composite policy. A portfolio that is above the minimum at the beginning of a
measurement period is included for that period in its entirety.

Where the firm changes a composite minimum, the change applies prospectively from the
beginning of the next measurement period (see also recommendation 3.B.1).

Where a portfolio falls below the minimum solely as a result of a significant cash
flow, the Board has addressed the treatment of such temporary breaches in
**Q&A QA-2025-031**.
