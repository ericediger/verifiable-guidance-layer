# Q&A QA-2025-031 — Temporary breach of a composite minimum following a significant cash flow

**Global Standards Board (GSB) — Interpretations Committee**
Issued 15 April 2025. Effective on issue.
Relates to provision 3.A.7 of the GSB Standards, Edition 2025, and Guidance Statement GS-2025-02.

## Question

Must a portfolio be removed from a composite when it falls below the composite
minimum solely because of a significant cash flow?

## Answer

No. Where the breach results solely from a significant cash flow, as defined in the
firm's documented policy for the composite, the portfolio may remain in the composite
for up to three full measurement periods following the cash flow, provided that the
firm's documented composite policy provides for this treatment and the determination
is documented at the time it is made. If the portfolio remains below the minimum at
the end of that period, it must be removed.

The Committee notes that this treatment is available only where the cause of the
breach is a significant cash flow. A portfolio that falls below the minimum through
market movement or through a series of cash flows that do not individually meet the
firm's significant cash flow threshold must be removed in accordance with 3.A.7.
