# Amendment 2025-A1 to the GSB Standards, Edition 2025 — Definitions

**Global Standards Board (GSB)**
Published 3 November 2026. Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 December 2026.

## Summary

Under definition 1.D.6 as published in Edition 2025, a portfolio remains fee-paying
when its fee is waived, so related-party portfolios — principals, employees, and
affiliates of the firm — with fees waived in full have been required to be included in
composites under provision 3.A.1. The Board has concluded that such portfolios are not
representative of the firm's fee-paying business and is amending definition 1.D.6 to
exclude them. No provision in Section 3 is amended.

## Amendment

Definition 1.D.6 is replaced in its entirety by the following:

**1.D.6 Fee-paying portfolio.** A fee-paying portfolio is one that is subject to the firm's
investment management fee schedule, whether or not the fee is paid directly from the
portfolio and whether or not the fee is reduced in part. Portfolios of related parties for
which all investment management fees are waived in full are not fee-paying portfolios.

## Transition

Firms must apply the amended definition from the effective date. Composite
membership determined under the previous definition for periods before the effective
date is not restated.
