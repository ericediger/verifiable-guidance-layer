# Amendment 2025-A1 to the GSB Standards, Edition 2025 — Definitions

**Global Standards Board (GSB)**
Published 3 November 2026. Effective for periods beginning on or after 1 December 2026.

## Summary

The Board has observed divergent practice in the treatment of portfolios of related
parties — principals, employees, and affiliates of the firm — for which investment
management fees are waived in full. Because the inclusion requirement in provision
3.A.1 turns on whether a portfolio is fee-paying, the Board is amending definition
1.D.6 to settle the matter. No provision in Section 3 is amended.

## Amendment

Definition 1.D.6 is replaced in its entirety by the following:

**1.D.6 Fee-paying portfolio.** A fee-paying portfolio is one for which the firm
receives compensation for investment management services, whether or not the fee is
paid directly from the portfolio. Portfolios of related parties for which all
investment management fees are waived in full are not fee-paying portfolios.

## Transition

Firms must apply the amended definition from the effective date. Composite
membership determined under the previous definition for periods before the effective
date is not restated.
